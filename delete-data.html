Delete your data

Lipstick & Lies: Affair Case · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Lipstick & Lies has no accounts and no login. Your progress is stored on your device. Google Firebase keeps analytics and crash data linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or e-mail.

Step 1: delete the data on your device

  1. Open the game and go to Settings → Delete data, then confirm. This erases your progress, currencies, choices and settings and starts the game fresh.
  2. Or uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Lipstick & Lies → Storage → Clear storage.

Step 2: ask us to delete analytics and crash data

  1. E-mail superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com with the subject "Lipstick & Lies data deletion".
  2. Include the player ID shown in Settings (or the date you last played and your device model).
  3. We delete the Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: game progress and settings on your device (step 1); Firebase Analytics events and user properties and Crashlytics reports for your app instance (step 2).
  • Kept by Google, not by us: purchase records (Google Play keeps them for payments, tax and refunds) and advertising data (manage it in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or at myaccount.google.com).
  • Without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.
