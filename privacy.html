Privacy Policy

Lipstick & Lies: Affair Case · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Lipstick & Lies: Affair Case" (the "Game") uses and why. The Game has no user accounts and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (cases, stars, choices, outfits, currencies), settings and a random player ID are saved on your device. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete data, or by uninstalling the Game.

2. No cloud storage of your progress

We do not run servers that store your progress. If you reinstall the Game, purchases can be restored from your store account with "Restore purchases"; your device backup (Google) may also restore local progress.

2a. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To see which scenes players enjoy or quit, and to fix crashes, the Game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Google acts as our service provider. They receive:

  • a random app-instance ID and, where allowed, the Android advertising ID; device model, operating system, language, app version and an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • gameplay events: cases and scenes started and finished, stars, hints, story choices, screens, offers shown, ads watched, and purchases (product, price and currency; never payment details);
  • crash reports (stack trace, device state and the last few gameplay events).

This data is not linked to your name or e-mail. Analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days. Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), your ad-consent choice also applies to Analytics' advertising features. To have this data deleted sooner, see Delete your data.

2b. Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you turn notifications on (the in-game prompt or Settings → Notifications), the Game registers a random push token with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and joins a few broad groups (for example language, how far you have played, whether you have made a purchase, and whether 18+ After Dark is switched on) so we can tell you about new cases and episodes. Turning notifications off in Settings deletes the token and leaves every group.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how ads are shown and interacted with, in order to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud.

  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Ads in the Game are not aimed at children, and the Game is intended for adults (18+).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we receive only a purchase receipt (product and order identifiers) to deliver what you bought. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your store account settings.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when energy is full or the daily riddle is ready). They are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us. Turn them off in Settings or in your device settings.

6. Updates

The Game checks Google Play for a newer version. This request contains only standard technical information (such as your IP address) and the Game's identifier.

7. Sharing

When you choose to share a result, the Game opens your device's share sheet with a short text and a store link. Nothing is posted automatically.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children and is intended for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your rights and contact

You can delete your data in the Game at any time (Settings → Delete data), and ask us to delete analytics and crash data as described on Delete your data. For any privacy question or request, contact us at superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com and include the player ID shown in Settings so we can help.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
